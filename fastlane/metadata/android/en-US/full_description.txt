Tell PackLog where you're going and when: it packs your suitcase with the clothes you already own, based on the weather, your stops and what you'll be doing. Then check items off as you pack.

AN AI-PACKED SUITCASE
• Add your trip's stops, what you'll do and where you'll stay: city, beach, hiking, work, a formal dinner.
• The AI picks clothes from your wardrobe based on the weather at each stop and the length of the trip.
• If there's a washing machine, you pack fewer clothes.
• See why each item is in your suitcase, and get a heads-up when the trip is missing something: a rain jacket, hiking boots.
• Change anything you like: remove, add, adjust quantities.

YOUR CLOTHES FROM A PHOTO
• Take a photo or pick one from your gallery: the AI recognizes each item.
• For every item it suggests type, colour, warmth and rain resistance. You review and save.
• No inventory to type by hand.

WEATHER FOR YOUR TRIP
• Temperatures, rain and wind for every stop.
• For trips far ahead it uses past years' averages, then switches to the forecast.

CHECKLIST AND LUGGAGE
• Check items off as you pack.
• Add toiletries, electronics and everything else from home.
• Set up your bags once (carry-on, backpack, tote) and reuse them on every trip.

YOUR THINGS, ACROSS HOMES AND SPACES
• Create the homes you need, yours, the beach house, your parents' place, and split them into spaces: wardrobe, drawers, cellar.
• Every item has its place and quantity, and shows as travelling when it leaves with you.

WORKS OFFLINE TOO
Your inventory and checklists live on your phone and work offline. When you're online, PackLog syncs everything to your account, so your things follow you to a new phone. AI packing, photo recognition and weather need a connection.
